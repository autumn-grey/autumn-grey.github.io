A passive investment planner for Torn, with full customisability and heapsa fun features

Prototype: some things might still be broken, currently only open to members of AutumnGrey's faction, a custom key is required to verify access. It's not saved unless you ask for it, and even then it's saved locally! This app doesn't play well with extensions like Dark Reader, so please disable for this site (it's already permanently in dark mode anyway). If you have suggestions or find a bug please message me, AutumnGrey directly.
This tool is a guide only. Prices and values are accurate at time of viewing but are subject to fluctuation. Purchasable job merits such as bank investment time and education length reductions are not accounted for. Stock sales profits are not considered for the purpose of this investment planner.
API Key

Use AutumnAPIAccessOnly to use the app with only public investment data. The app is set with default settings and can be manually configured and updated by the user.

Use AutumnAPICustomised to preconfigure settings with your personal statistics including, stocks owned, available funds, job perks, merits, and education courses studied.

Click your preferred access level to generate a new custom key, then paste it below

All keys and saved data are stored locally on your device, this app sends no data, and saves no sensitive information.
