Work Stat Export

This collects your company work stat history into one anonymous file, to help work out exactly how Torn calculates the work stats you gain each day.

What is pulled from your logs

  • Your daily company pay, which records the work stats and job points you gained
  • Your position changes, and when you joined, quit or were fired from each company
  • Every other work stat gain: director trains, finished courses, city job pay, the Working 9 Til 5 book and job or company specials
  • Your merit assignments, for the Employee Effectiveness merit
  • Your drug use, overdoses, rehab and addiction removal, because addiction lowers effectiveness

And from your account

  • Your current work stats, and the type and star rating of your current company
  • Your position, days in the company and effectiveness breakdown
  • The type of each company you worked for, and the requirements and listed gains of the positions you held

Kept anonymous

Your name and player ID are not included. Company IDs are replaced with Company 1, Company 2 and so on, and the IDs of other players and your faction are removed from every log entry.

Nothing else is read. Your key is used once, in your browser, to ask Torn for the above, and is never saved or sent anywhere else.
