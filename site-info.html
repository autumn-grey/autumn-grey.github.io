Autumn's Stonking Awesome Torn App

Reference for autumn-grey.github.io · v0.5.6 · Updated: 2026-09-03

A passive-investment planner for Torn. The app itself is a single self-contained HTML file, hosted on GitHub Pages, with no backend and nothing to install.

Currently restricted to members of The ZOO - Night Shift.

The version number and last-updated date sit in the footer, and the version is stamped into every export, so a bug report can be tied to an exact build.

Getting started

  1. Open autumn-grey.github.io in your browser.
  2. Pick a key type from the API Key panel. Clicking either name opens Torn with the key's name and permissions already filled in: - AutumnAPIAccessOnly. Public investment data only. Everything is set to defaults and configured by hand. - AutumnAPICustomised. Also reads your own stats and preconfigures the settings for you.
  3. Create the key on Torn, paste it into the box, and press Refresh all API data.
  4. Press Save Key/Settings if you want it remembered next time.

Your key is checked against the faction before anything else loads. Nothing works if you're not a member of The ZOO - Night Shift, or a staff member.

What the customised key fills in

Stocks you own · cash on hand · City Bank balance and time left on the deposit · Cayman balance · bank merits · education merits · courses studied · job perks (Fat Cat, Tax Haven, education course time) · faction Cayman bonus · Private Island rent you pay or charge · WSU block ownership · whether you direct a company (ticks TCP, TGP and SYS) · whether you have raced (ticks TCM) · job point specials from your company's star rating (Oil Mogul, Healthy Mind, Cutting Corners, Interior Connections).

Anything it can't read stays on its default, and the results panel lists every setting line by line so you can see what worked. Open Diagnostics inside it if something looks wrong. It shows exactly what your key returned, including the raw bank rates and the City Bank fields.

Every setting stays editable afterwards. Preconfiguring is a starting point, not a lock.

Saved market data

Share prices, item values, bank rates and the reference tables are kept in your browser after each refresh and restored when you come back, so a reload is instant and costs no API calls. The status line says how old the saved figures are. Personal data is never cached.

The two pages

Investments

A table of every stock benefit block, priced and ranked by return. Share prices come from tornsy.com; items, bank rates and the points market come from Torn's API.

  • Basic view is the simplified one: fewer columns, abbreviated money, colour-coded rows, and a break-even column. Best on a phone.
  • Advanced view adds the full column set and the configuration panels down the left.
  • Click a row once to mark it owned, twice to skip it, and three times to clear it. Owned rows are outlined; skipped rows get a dashed outline and are left out of recommendations, but still appear in the planner. Marking a higher increment marks everything below it; skipping a lower one skips everything above it; clearing a lower one clears everything above it.
  • The Plan column ticks feed the planner's Custom Plan path.
  • The Portfolio panel totals what you own and what you have ticked to plan.
  • Stocks with no measurable return live in their own Undefined ROI table underneath.

Planner

Works out the order to buy things in, and how long each step will take. Nothing recalculates until you press Show Me The Money (Plan), because the simulation is heavy and live updates made the settings unusable.

  • Your Position. Available capital, daily investment budget, City Bank payout, days remaining on the deposit, and the term length. The daily budget is a starting figure; it grows as the investments you buy start paying out. Term length is set manually, with a best guess pre-selected.
  • Path. One of Everything, a certain investment, Custom Plan, 1000 Energy, just give me money, increment amount, or Total Newbie (see below).
  • Other Investments. Private Islands, City Bank, Cayman and TCI.
  • Situational Stocks. Tick the ones whose benefits you'd actually use. Unticked ones are bought last and never incremented. Stocks with no calculable ROI sit below a divider; click one's name to pin it as the goal the plan works toward first. Pinned stocks are bought cheapest first.
  • Steps are numbered, tickable, and paged (20/50/100/500 per page). Jump to current step finds the next thing you should buy, switching pages if it's on another one.
  • Indented steps with amber lines are bought to earn money while you save for something bigger, one line per level of nesting.
  • Red steps want selling: either a holding that's worth less than what the money could be doing, or something you ticked out of order.
  • The Portfolio panel underneath tracks what you own as the plan progresses.
  • An amber note appears under the button when a setting has changed since the plan was built. Dismiss it with the × and it stays gone.

Job point specials

Two company specials spend job points to shorten a timer, and both are now modelled.

  • Oil Mogul (Oil Rig 5★). 3 job points take an hour off a City Bank investment. The term still pays its full interest, so the same money arrives sooner and the effective APR rises. TCI's return rises with it.
  • Healthy Mind (Fitness Centre 1★) and Cutting Corners (Hair Salon 7★). 1 job point takes 30 minutes off a course, which shortens the study time WSU and IST are valued against.
  • Interior Connections (Property Broker 10★). 10% off property upgrades, stacking additively with an owned ELT block for 20%.

Job points per day equal your company's star rating, so a refresh sets these from your job. Each dropdown says how much time it saves per day.

Total Newbie

A path that plans from a standing start, with the things a new player doesn't have yet:

  • No bank merits, no perks, no education.
  • City Bank terms unlock one at a time: 1 week straight away, then each longer term once a full term of the one before it has run (2 weeks at day 7, 1 month at day 21, 2 months at day 51, 3 months at day 111), starting from whatever you have already unlocked.
  • Bank merits arrive on their own: 6 at one week, 8 at two, 10 at three.
  • Computer Science courses are assumed finished at month 12, so I Industries Ltd. isn't bought before then.
  • Property Law is assumed finished at two years, so a Private Island is priced with its 10% discount and not bought earlier.

Exporting

The download icon above either table saves a .tsv file. Tab-separated, plain ASCII, integers only, so it opens cleanly in Excel and Google Sheets anywhere in the world: no comma-versus-semicolon problem, no mangled characters, no dates invented out of decimals. The investments export puts both tables in one file with a totals row; the planner export covers the whole plan, not just the page on screen, along with the settings that produced it.

That settings footer is what makes a plan export usable as a bug report: path, increments, capital, income, bank state, situational ticks and every row you marked.

See Planner Logic for the full reasoning: how an investment is valued, how the order is chosen, and what the simulation does day by day.

Your data

Everything lives in your own browser. The app talks to Torn's API and tornsy.com and nowhere else. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is stored unless you press Save. The full terms are on the Terms of Service page, linked from the footer.

If something goes wrong, a red panel appears under the API status listing every problem with a timestamp, and a Copy button that hands the whole log over as text for a bug report.

Known limitations

  • Prices are assumed to hold. The planner projects forward from today's share prices. Real ones move.
  • Stock sale profits are not considered. Only benefits are valued.
  • Torn doesn't report bank term lengths, only the time left, so the term is inferred and can be set by hand.
  • Private Island upgrade costs are hardcoded. Torn's API doesn't expose them. Benefit requirements, property prices, course lengths and course prices are all pulled live, with the built-in tables as fallbacks.
  • Torn reports modifications only to a property's owner, so the upgrade state of an island you rent in can't be read and isn't checked.
  • Job details come from API v2. Torn's v1 refuses the job selection outright.
